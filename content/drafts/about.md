Trips at Travel Devils aren't just itineraries. They turn into inside jokes, louder laughter, new friendships and stories you'll tell long after the bags are unpacked.

We're a travel community for people who'd rather be in the mountains than stuck in the 9-to-5 loop: weekend getaways from Delhi, Himalayan backpacking trips, treks, international group tours, corporate offsites and fully customised holidays.

## Who we are

Travel Devils is a growing tribe of adventure seekers exploring the Himalayas and beyond. We design fun, affordable and well-planned trips, whether you're travelling solo, with friends or with your whole crew.

College days shape who we become: the values, the dreams and the fire to build something of our own. Those same values brought together a group of travel-obsessed explorers who wanted to change how young India travels. The result is a travel community people genuinely connect with.


## How our trips work

- **Fixed group departures.** Most of our trips leave every week, so you can pick a date that suits you and join a group of like-minded travellers.
- **A trip captain on every group.** An experienced captain travels with you and handles the plan, the stays and the small details so you can switch off.
- **Groups of 8 to 50.** Big enough for the party, small enough that nobody gets lost in the crowd.
- **Solo? You're in good company.** Many of our travellers join solo and leave with friends.
- **Everything listed upfront.** Stays, travel, sightseeing and meals are written out on every trip page, with inclusions and exclusions, so you know exactly what you pay for.
- **Your trip, your way.** Want different dates, a private group or a hotel upgrade? [Plan a custom trip](#plan-my-trip) and we'll send you a quotation.

## What we value

**Community.** You meet a lot of people in a lifetime. We want the ones you meet on our trips to add something real to yours: every traveller who goes with us becomes part of the Travel Devils community.

**Looking after travellers.** Our founders started out in the same 9-to-5 routine as many of our travellers. Having been in your shoes, we know what a great trip needs to feel like, and we work to deliver exactly that, and a little more.

**Transparency.** Our team, our local partners and our travellers work towards the same goal, and that only works when everyone knows exactly what's happening. Clear prices, clear inclusions, clear policies.

**Creativity.** When your job is a new adventure every week, doing things the usual way is a setback. From trip captains to our sales team, everyone here adds their own spin.

**Freedom from hassles.** Life is full of rulebooks. On our trips we take care of the logistics so you get to experience the raw, unplanned joy of travel.
