## Prices

- Prices on the website are **per person** in Indian Rupees and depend on the sharing type (for example triple or quad sharing), route and package you choose.
- **GST** is added at checkout where applicable and shown as a separate line before you pay.
- Coupon discounts are applied at checkout and shown before payment. One coupon per booking.

## Paying online

Online payments are processed securely by **Razorpay** (cards, UPI, net banking and wallets). We never see or store your card details.

When you book online you can:

1. **Pay in full**, or
2. **Reserve with the booking amount** shown on the trip page (per traveller) and pay the balance later from [My Bookings](/account).

Your seat is confirmed as soon as the payment succeeds. You'll see your booking ID on the confirmation page and in My Bookings.

## Balance payment

The balance must be paid **[[CONFIRM: e.g. at least 7 days before departure]]**. If the balance isn't paid by then, we may release your seat, and the booking amount is kept as per our [Cancellation Policy](/cancellation-policy).

## Failed or duplicate payments

If money was deducted but your booking isn't confirmed, don't pay again. Email [info@traveldevils.in](mailto:info@traveldevils.in) with the payment reference. Failed payments are usually reversed by your bank automatically within 5–7 working days; duplicate payments are refunded in full (see our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy)).
