## Frequently asked questions

**What is Travel Devils?**
Travel Devils brings India's travellers together through community-based, experiential trips. From solo adventures to squad getaways, we design journeys that hit both the heart and the bucket list.

**Who can join Travel Devils trips?**
Anyone! Solo travellers, couples, friends and first-time travellers are all welcome.

**Can solo travellers join group trips?**
Absolutely. Many of our travellers join solo and make lifelong friends along the way.

**What is the group size?**
It varies by trip but is usually between 8 and 50 travellers.

**What is included in the trip package?**
Inclusions typically cover accommodation, transport, sightseeing, a trip captain and select meals. Exact inclusions and exclusions are listed on every trip page.

**Is it safe to travel with Travel Devils?**
Safety is our top priority. Every trip is led by an experienced trip captain and run with trusted local partners.

**Can I customise my trip?**
Yes. Both domestic and international trips can be tailored to your dates, group and budget. [Plan a custom trip](#plan-my-trip).

**How do I cancel or change a booking?**
Email [info@traveldevils.in](mailto:info@traveldevils.in) with your booking ID. Our team will confirm any cancellation charges and refund timelines for your booking.
