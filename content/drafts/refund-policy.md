This policy explains when you're eligible for a refund and how long it takes.

## When refunds apply

- **You cancel:** you're refunded whatever you've paid above the charges in our [Cancellation Policy](/cancellation-policy). The booking amount isn't refundable.
- **We cancel a departure:** you get a full refund of the amount paid to us, or you can move to another date.
- **A payment went through twice, or the amount was wrong:** we refund the extra amount in full.
- **You paid but the departure filled up at the same moment:** we'll offer you another date or a full refund.

## What isn't refundable

- No-shows, and services you didn't use once the trip started (for example leaving early or skipping an activity).
- Activities or sightseeing cancelled because of weather, road or government restrictions (we try to offer an alternative).
- Charges levied by airlines, hotels, railways, embassies or other suppliers that are non-refundable on their side. These are deducted from your refund.

## How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method** (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) through our payment partner, Razorpay. Payments you made by bank transfer or UPI directly to us are refunded to the same account.
- Approved refunds are processed within **7–14 working days** of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
- We'll confirm the refund amount and date by email.

## Returns

Travel Devils sells travel services, not physical goods, so product returns don't apply.
