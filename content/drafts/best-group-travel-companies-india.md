Group trips are the easiest way to see the Himalayas, the North-East or South-East Asia without planning every bus, stay and permit yourself. You pay one price, travel with a trip captain and a group of like-minded people, and come back with new friends. But the operators differ a lot in what they run, how long the trips are and what "all-inclusive" really means.

## How to choose a group travel company

Whichever operator you go with, ask these before paying:

1. **What exactly is included?** Check meals (how many), transport type, permits, and whether GST is extra.
2. **How long is the trip, door to door?** A "7-day" trip that starts with an overnight bus has fewer days at the destination.
3. **Who leads the group?** Ask if a trip captain travels with you for the whole trip.
4. **What's the cancellation policy?** Read the slabs before you pay the booking amount.
5. **How big is the group?** Sizes vary a lot between operators and batches.
6. **What happens if weather closes a road?** Mountain itineraries change; ask how the operator handles it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best group travel company in India?**
It depends on the trip. For weekly weekend departures from Delhi, Travel Devils; for the widest choice of Himalayan treks and biking trips, JustWravel; for the most international destinations, Go4Explore; for community and festival trips, WanderOn. Compare the specific trip you want.

**Are group trips cheaper than planning on your own?**
Often, for remote places like Spiti or Ladakh, because transport, stays and permits are shared across the group. For easy city breaks, planning yourself can cost less.

**Is GST included in group trip prices?**
Usually not. All three operators whose Winter Spiti pages we could read (including us) add 5% GST on top of the displayed price.

**Can I join a group trip solo?**
Yes. Most travellers on these trips join solo or in pairs.
