## How to cancel

Send your cancellation request in writing to [info@traveldevils.in](mailto:info@traveldevils.in) with your booking ID (for example TD-1A2B3C4) and the names of the travellers cancelling. The cancellation date is the date we receive your email. Phone or WhatsApp messages alone aren't treated as a cancellation.

## Cancellation charges (group trips)

The **booking amount** you paid to reserve your seat is **non-refundable** for any cancellation. On top of that, the following charges apply to the total trip cost:

| When you cancel | Cancellation charge |
|---|---|
| More than 30 days before departure | 50% of the trip cost |
| 15–30 days before departure | 75% of the trip cost |
| Less than 15 days before departure, or no-show | 100% of the trip cost |

Anything you've paid above the applicable charge is refunded as described in our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).

## Custom trips, flights and special bookings

- **Custom and private trips:** charges depend on the hotels, transport and activities booked for you, and are shared with your quotation.
- **Flights, trains and visas:** charged as per the airline, railway or embassy rules; these fees are usually non-refundable.
- **Promotional or discounted fares:** some offers are non-refundable. Where that applies, it's stated on the offer.

## If we change or cancel a trip

- If we cancel a departure (for example because of low numbers, weather, road closures or government restrictions), you can choose another date or a **full refund of the amount paid** to us.
- Trip captains may change the itinerary on the spot for safety, weather, road or permit conditions. Sightseeing or activities missed because of weather, landslides, traffic or other events outside our control aren't refundable, but we'll always try to offer an alternative.
- We may remove a traveller from a trip, without a refund, for serious misconduct, illegal activity or putting others at risk (see [Terms & Conditions](/terms-and-condition)).
