## Overview

A 5-7 day backpacking trip to Meghalaya, the "Abode of Clouds," offers an intense experience of lush landscapes, living root bridges, and waterfalls, best visited between September and December. Key highlights include trekking to the Double Decker Root Bridge in Nongriat, exploring Dawki's crystal-clear river, visiting Asia's cleanest village, Mawlynnong, and caving in Cherrapunji.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival at Guwahati – Shillong

- Upon arrival at Guwahati Airport, the representative will meet and greet the group.[7]
- The group will head to Shillong at 12 PM.[7]
- It is requested to book a flight arriving before 11 am at Guwahati Airport.[7]
- The scenic drive from Guwahati to Shillong takes 4 hours.[7]
- You will see the Umiam Lake (Barapani) on the way, and after capturing its beauty, the journey will continue.[8]
- Check-in at the hotel, and the evening will be spent at Police Bazar, Shillong.
- Overnight stay at Shillong.


### Day 2: Shillong - Cherrapunji via Laitlum Canyon

Enjoy a lip-smacking breakfast.

Get transferred to Cherrapunji.

On the way, visit the breathtaking Laitlum Canyon and Mawkdok Dympep Viewpoint.

After this, grasp a stunning view of Nohkalikai falls, the Garden of caves, Arwah Caves, and Seven Sisters' fall if time permits.

Overnight stay at Cherrapunji.


### Day 3: Cherrapunji - Nongriat Trek - Cherrapunji

After breakfast at 7 am, proceed to Tyrna village.

Start the trek to the Double Decker Root Bridge (Nongriat) with the local guide.

The hike involves climbing up the 3500 steps and ends back at the same village by evening.

Overnight stay at Cherrapunji.


### Day 4: Cherrapunji - Mawlynnong - Dawki

Again, rush to the breakfast area.

Get ready and leave for the Dill Chahta hai Fort and make plans there to visit Goa daily.

We will also do cycling right at the Para Road (Dear Zindagi).

Visit those aesthetic colorful homes, talk to locals, know their culture and slow life in Goa.

The evening will be at Dauna Paula watching the most beautiful sunset of your life.

End our day in Casino or Tittos lane in Baga beach. Both Entry fees are not included in the package.

Return to Hotel.


### Day 5: Shnongpdeng - Jowai – Shillong

- Wake up to see the beauty of East Khasi Hills, and have breakfast.
- You can do multiple adventure activities (on your own) like Kayaking, Boating, Ziplining, and Cliff jumping, etc., over here.
- Start for Jowai to visit Krang Suri Waterfalls, which is described as India's most magical waterfall.
- You can get drenched and swim in the natural pools at the waterfall.
- Travel back to Shillong after witnessing these attractive places.
- Overnight stay at Shillong.


### Day 6: Shillong - Guwahati Airport Drop

Morning after breakfast, the group heads towards Guwahati Airport around 9 am.

It's a 4-5 hours drive to the airport, and the group is expected to reach the Airport by 3 pm.

Kindly book your flights post 5 pm.

Bid farewell to the trip.

Note: It is advised to spend a maximum of 25-40 minutes at one sightseeing place to cover all mentioned locations.
