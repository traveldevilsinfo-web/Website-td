## Overview

Kanatal is a small village that is a hill station in the state of Uttarakhand, India. Kanatal is 78 km from Dehradun, 38 km from Mussoorie and 18 km from Chamba. It is on the Chamba-Mussoorie road and nearly 300 km from Delhi.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Adventure activites & surkanda devi temple visit

Reach Kanatal & have breakfast.
After check-in into rooms, get fresh n up for Adventure Activities,
and visit to Dhanaulti Eco Park & surkanda Devi Mandir.
After that back to property & enjoy evening snacks and tea.
Later enjoy dinner at night with bonfire and light music.


### Day 2: TEHRI LAKE (WATER SPORTS ACTIVITIES)

Wake up early morning and enjoy the beautiful morning view of kanatal, Refresh & have breakfast.
After Breakfast we will start our journey to Tehri lake for adventure activities. It will take around 1 hour drive to tehri lake.
We can enjoy various adventure activities there like Jet Skiing,
Banana ride, jet attack, Speed boat and much more on direct payment basis.
After leaving tehri lake, we will depart for Rishikesh for Ganga Aarti (depending upon time) and local sightseeing in rishikesh.
We will reach Delhi by Early Morning.
