## Overview

Auli, located in Uttarakhand's Chamoli district, is India's premier skiing destination, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayas, including Nanda Devi. Situated at 2,500m to 3,050m, this "meadow" features long slopes ideal for skiing (January–March) and the Asia's second-longest cable car, making it a popular year-round hill station.
