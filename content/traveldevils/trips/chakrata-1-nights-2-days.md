## Overview

Chakrata is a cantonment town and also a sub district/tehsil, in Dehradun district in the state of Uttarakhand, India. It lies between the Tons and Yamuna rivers, at an elevation of 2118 m, 98 km from the state capital, Dehradun. Chakrata was originally a cantonment of the British Indian Army.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Reach Chakrata | Bhudher Cave Trek

Reach Chakrata in morning. Check-in the resort, rest for a while.
After some rest, drive towards Budher cave (a hike of 5km).
In evening, return back to resort.
In evening enjoy bonfire with snacks and some music. Conclude
with dinner and overnight stay.


### Day 2: Visit to tiger waterfall & chirmiri neck top

After breakfast, check out from resort and visit highest waterfalls
in Uttarakhand, known as Tiger Falls.
Later, move towards Chirmiri Neck Top, enjoy scenic sunset. and
afterwards depart for Dellhi.
Reach Delhi by early morning.
