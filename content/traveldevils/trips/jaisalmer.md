## Overview

A 2-3 day trip to Jaisalmer, the "Golden City" of Rajasthan, offers a magical blend of history and desert adventure, best visited between October and March. Highlights include exploring the 12th-century living Jaisalmer Fort, ornate havelis, and experiencing overnight desert camping with camel safaris, cultural folk music.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: REACH JAISALMER | CITY TOUR

Upon reaching Jaisalmer, Get ready for a Guided tour of Jaisalmer - Golden City.
Visit the golden Jaisalmer Fort that looks like it has just risen from the sands with its golden limestone walls and ancient havelis of Jaisalmer.
Take a short stopover at the beautiful Gadisar Lake that was once the only source of water in Jaisalmer.
Overnight stay at Hotel in Jaisalmer.


### Day 2: LONGEWALA BATTLEFIELD

Wake up at your hotel to a beautiful morning and enjoy a delicious breakfast.
Later, get ready to visit the border town of Longewala, the spot for Indo- Pak war of 1971. Longewala is a place of historical significance that serves as a reminder of the bravery and sacrifice of Indian soldiers during a critical moment in the nation's history.
In the evening, enjoy folk performances by the locals while enjoying a bonfire. Have your wholesome Rajasthani meal with the fellow travelers. Overnight stay at the Desert Camp in Jaisalmer.


### Day 3: JAISALMER CITY TOUR TO DEPARTURE

Wake up in the morning, enjoy a hearty breakfast and check out from the campsite.
Later get ready for Camel ride on Sam Sand Dunes & Jeep Safari and you can experience the spectacular view of Sunrise in Thar desert.
Post lunch, get ready to get chills in bone as you explore abandoned village of Kuldhara.
Later, depart for Delhi around 4-5PM. Reach Delhi the next morning with beautiful trip memories.
