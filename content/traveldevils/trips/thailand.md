## Overview

The gorgeous beaches that surround Phuket, which is nestled in the lap of the great Andaman Sea, have long worked like a charm for all beach lovers. Because it is Thailand's largest island, it is also the most active in the country.

The Sino-colonial palaces, as well as Big Buddha and Wat Chalong, offer a glimpse of Phuket's eclectic culture. When you visit Phi Phi Island, which was featured in Leonardo Dicaprio's hit film The Beach, share the screens of Hollywood movies.
Seeing the beautiful limestone karsts rising skyward from the sea, as well as the soothing flora that covers the majority of the island, provides a sense of tranquilly that one can only imagine. When you witness the waves breaking on the breathtakingly gorgeous coral reefs, you'll be lost in the splendour of Phi Phi Island.

While you're here, take advantage of the different things the area has to offer to keep your adrenaline pumping. Visit the Thai island of Koh Phangan for a colourful 7-day tour that includes a full moon party, snorkelling, and adventure activities like zip line and snorkelling.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Phuket | Transfer To Krabi

Arrive at Phuket Airport and get assisted at the immigration counter for a Thailand tourist Visa.
A representative will meet you and accompany you to your Krabi hotel (approx. 3-hour road journey).
Check-in and spend the evening at Ao Nang beach, and visit Krabi's nightlife (by your own).
Overnight stay at the Krabi Hotel.


### Day 2: 4 Island Tour By Speed Boat | Stay at Krabi

Have breakfast at the hotel.
Take a 4 Island tour by Speedboat from Krabi to watch the sunset.
Visit Chicken Island, Koh Poda, Tup Island, and Cave Beaches.
Enjoy a walk on the sandbar and snorkel in the turquoise waters.
Overnight stay in Krabi.


### Day 3: Koh Samui | Exploring the Island Full Moon Party

After breakfast, transfer to Koh Samui on a SIC (Seat-In-Coach) basis.
Check into rooms and change into beachwear.
Attend the Overnight Full Moon Party at Koh Phangan Beach.


### Day 4: A Leisure Day in Koh Samui

This day is for rejuvenating after the full night party.
Transfer from Haad Rinn beach to Koh Samui via Speedboat.
Enjoy the beach surroundings and zestful environment.
Overnight stay at Koh Samui.


### Day 5: Koh Samui To Phuket | City Walk And Pub Crawl

Experience the scenic beauty and oceanic route from Koh Samui to Phuket by ferry.
The transfer includes Koh Samui pier to Surat Thani pier (1 hour) and Surat Thani to Phuket hotel by van (4.5 hours).
In the evening, wander through the most famous spot in Phuket: Bangla Walking Street and Patong Beach.
Enjoy beach shows and the happening street.
Visit Illuzion club, ranked among the world's top 13 clubs.
Overnight stay at Phuket.


### Day 6: Phi-Phi Island Tour By Speed Boat | Overnight Stay at Phuket

After breakfast, the theme is Sunning, Swimming, and Snorkeling.
Morning pick-up from the hotel is between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM.
Visit the Phi Phi Islands.
The itinerary includes the stunning Phi Leh Lagoon, surrounded by limestone cliffs, and Maya Bay (famous from the movie 'The Beach').
Complete the trip by snorkeling among native fish and coral gardens.
After the island time, hit the city's night clubs to enjoy the party culture.
Overnight stay at a Phuket hotel.


### Day 7: Departure from Phuket Airport

Take your ride from Phuket to catch your flights.
