## Overview

The two places are approximately 50-60 km from each other, making them an ideal trip for pilgrims and nature lovers. Mukteshwar is famous for its 350-year-old Shiva temple and panoramic views of the Himalayas, while Kainchi Dham is home to the ashram of Neem Karoli Baba, known for its serenity.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Mukteshwar

Morning: Arrive in Mukteshwar and check into your camps or rooms.
Afternoon: Head out for a short 1.5 km hike to a local waterfall. After the hike, visit Chauli ki Jali and the Mukteshwar Temple, renowned for their breathtaking views and cultural significance.
Evening: Return to your accommodation for a relaxing bonfire and music session. Dinner will be served, followed by an overnight stay.


### Day 2: Adventure and Sightseeing

Morning: After breakfast, check out and engage in thrilling adventure activities.
Afternoon: Visit Kainchi Dham, a revered site known for its spiritual ambiance. Later, proceed to either Nainital or Bhimtal for some leisurely sightseeing.
Evening: Depart for Delhi at 9:00 PM.
Next Day Early Morning: Arrival in Delhi
