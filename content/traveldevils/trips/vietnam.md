## Overview

Vietnam is a Southeast Asian country known for its rich history, diverse culture, and stunning natural landscapes. Stretching along the eastern coast of the Indochina Peninsula, it shares borders with China, Laos, and Cambodia, and has a long coastline along the South China Sea.

The country is famous for its mix of bustling cities and serene countryside. Hanoi, the capital, is known for its centuries-old architecture, lakes, and vibrant street food scene. Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Saigon) is the economic hub, offering a mix of French colonial landmarks and modern skyscrapers.

Culturally, Vietnam blends traditions influenced by China, France, and indigenous heritage. It's home to numerous UNESCO World Heritage Sites such as Ha Long Bay, Hoi An Ancient Town, and My Son Sanctuary. Festivals like Tet (Lunar New Year) are celebrated with great enthusiasm across the country.

Nature is another highlight: from the emerald waters and limestone cliffs of Ha Long Bay to terraced rice fields in Sapa, the sandy beaches of Da Nang and Nha Trang, and the lush Mekong Delta in the south.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Ho Chi Minh

Link up with your squad and dive into the colorful chaos of Ben Thanh Market.
Fuel up with Pho and the silky smooth Egg Coffee.
As night falls, head to Bui Vien Street, the ultimate backpacker hotspot, for a wild night out.


### Day 2: Cu Chi Tunnels Full Day Tour

Step back in time to explore the legendary Cu Chi Tunnels, an underground maze that brings Vietnam War history to life.
Channel your inner action hero at the shooting range with real AK-47s and M16s.
Continue the journey at the War Remnants Museum for a powerful, eye-opening look into Vietnam's past.


### Day 3: Danang: Beach vibes, Breathtaking views

Have breakfast and transfer to the Airport.
After reaching Danang, collect your baggage and head towards your hotel at Mekai beach.
Check into the hotel and stroll at the scenic Mekai beach.
Later in the evening, you can explore the Dragon bridge by yourself.


### Day 4: Ba Na Hills Adventure

Step into the dreamlike world of Ba Na Hills.
Soar above the clouds on one of the world's longest cable car rides.
Cross the stunning Golden Hand Bridge, a must-see Instagram hotspot.
Wander through the charming French Village, transporting you straight to Europe.
As evening arrives, return to Danang to dive into the bustling night market for street food and local treats.


### Day 5: Bamboo Basket Boat Ride & Hoi An Ancient Town Tour

The morning is all yours to explore Hoi An, whether you want to shop, get a custom-made outfit, or simply enjoy the local vibe.
In the afternoon, get ready for an exciting adventure on traditional bamboo basket boats.
For a magical evening, take a peaceful lantern boat ride on the river for just $2 to $3.
End the day with a relaxing walk through Hoi An's charming ancient town and discover its lovely cafés.


### Day 6: Explore Hanoi's Old Quarter and Experience the Excitement of Train Street

Fly to Hanoi, Vietnam's vibrant capital.
Stroll through the bustling Old Quarter while enjoying a cup of authentic Vietnamese coffee.
Experience the famous Train Street, where trains roll right through a narrow neighborhood.


### Day 7: Halong Bay Cruise with Kayaking and Cave Exploration

Sail through the stunning Halong Bay, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
Kayak through hidden lagoons and explore ancient caves.
Enjoy a delicious lunch on a traditional junk boat.
End the day with a lively party at Hanoi's vibrant Beer Street.

### Day 8: Departure Day: Until Next Time!

Enjoy one last breakfast with the crew before it's time to say goodbye.
