## Overview

Chopta is a serene, high-altitude meadow in Uttarakhand, India, serving as the base for trekking to [Tungnath](https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&rls=en&q=Tungnath&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&ved=2ahUKEwiYwZTbidKSAxXv0TQHHVO9HzYQgK4QegYIAQgAEAs), the world’s highest Shiva temple (3,680m), and the [Chandrashila summit](https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&rls=en&q=Chandrashila+summit&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&ved=2ahUKEwiYwZTbidKSAxXv0TQHHVO9HzYQgK4QegYIAQgAEAw) (3,960m). Often called the "Mini Switzerland of India," it is a popular year-round trekking destination, best visited from April to June for lush greenery or January to March for snow.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Devprayag Sangam | Chopta Arrival

Stop at Devprayag to witness the confluence of Bhagirathi & Alaknanda rivers.
After breakfast near Rudraprayag, proceed to Chopta. Check-in, relax, take a forest walk, enjoy a bonfire in the evening, and have a home-style dinner.


### Day 2: Adventure and Sightseeing

Early morning, trek to Tungnath and Chandrashila Peak (weather permitting). Return to Chopta, relax, and stay overnight.


### Day 3: Drive To Sari Village| Trek To Deoriatal

After breakfast, drive to Sari Village, trek to Deoriatal Lake, and return to Sari.
Begin the return journey to Delhi with a stop in Rishikesh for exploration, cafe hopping, and visiting temples and Ganga ghats (if time permits).
Reach Delhi next day Early morning.
