## Overview

A trip to Udaipur and Kumbhalgarh offers a captivating blend of royal history, serene lakes, and stunning architecture in the Aravalli hills of Rajasthan. Udaipur is known for its romantic ambiance and palaces, while Kumbhalgarh is famous for its formidable, UNESCO World Heritage-listed fort.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Reach UDAIPUR | CITY TOUR

Reach Udaipur, and Check-in at the hotel. Relax, and freshen up at the hotel.
Post this, leave for Udaipur sightseeing.
Today we will visit Saheliyon ki Bari & Fateh Sagar Lake.
Explore Fateh Sagar Lake market where you can try Udaipur's local cuisine.
Reach back to the hotel.
Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.


### Day 2: UDAIPUR SIGHTSEEING

After a delicious breakfast, leave for Udaipur local sightseeing.
Today, we will visit Lake Pichola, City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Gangaur Ghat.
In the evening, either you can enjoy the culture & folk art performance at Bagore Ki Haveli(7-8pm) or you can explore local flea market.
Reach back to the hotel.
Dinner will be served. Overnight stay at the hotel.


### Day 3: KUMBHALGARH FORT & DEPARTURE

Wake up in the morning.
Have your breakfast & check out from the hotel.
Post this, leave for the famous Kumbhalgarh Fort.
Kumbhalgarh Fort – one of the most beautiful forts in Rajasthan and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The walls of the fort extend over 38 km and is the second longest wall after the Great Wall of China.
Departure from Kumbhalgarh by evening.
Overnight journey to Gurugram. Halt for dinner (Not on us).
