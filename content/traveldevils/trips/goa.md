## Overview

Goa is India's smallest state by area, but one of its most popular travel destinations, known for its beaches, vibrant nightlife, and Portuguese heritage. Located on the western coast of India along the Arabian Sea, it attracts millions of visitors each year for its unique mix of relaxation, adventure, and culture.
Historically, Goa was a Portuguese colony for over 450 years, which is reflected in its architecture, churches, cuisine, and laid-back lifestyle. Old Goa is home to UNESCO World Heritage churches like the Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral, which showcase baroque and Portuguese influences.

from The coastline is dotted with beaches that cater to different moods lively and party-driven stretches like Baga, Calangute, and Anjuna, to quieter, scenic ones like Palolem, Agonda, and Morjim. Water sports such as parasailing, jet skiing, and scuba diving are also popular.
Goa is equally famous for its nightlife and music scene, with beach shacks, nightclubs, and electronic music festivals drawing both domestic and international crowds. The state is also known for its flea markets, yoga retreats, and wellness resorts.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Departure from Pune/Bangalore

The group assembles at the pick-up point. You'll meet the team captains, and a small tour briefing session would be provided. You can also join directly in Goa.


### Day 2: Goa with strangers

We will check in to our hotel and will be welcomed by a delicious lunch.

We will rush to the nearest beach because "beach dekhne hee to aaye the".

Back to the hotel for the pool party.

Note: Wear Shades of blue or green for the pool Party.

Post that, we will leave for clubbing in any one of the famous clubs of Goa.

Back to the hotel by early morning, say around 3.


### Day 3: Offbeat plus market day

Wake up and run towards the breakfast area.

We will first go to Arambol beach, start our 20-minute hike crossing a not-at-all dense forest, and reach a beautiful lake.

Take a dip there, relax for a while, and trekking enthusiasts can go trek more and discover hidden spots. Trust me, they are more beautiful than this beautiful lake.

Later on, we will cover the Goa local market, do some shopping, buy hippy stuff, and later regret it.

Relax for a while near the beach, as the day is about to end, and now you have 3 options:
Option 1: Dance like anything in a nearby shack in Arambol beach.
Option 2: Let's decorate our picnic spot right in front of the sea (a bedsheet, some scented candles, give an aesthetic vibe), look at the stars, talk about lives, or just stay quiet and hear the sea talk.
Option 3: We will go to Titos Lane.

We will plan according to the vibe and mood of the group, or what if we can tick both out of our bucket list.

Back to the hotel.


### Day 4: Not so offbeat

Again, rush to the breakfast area.

Get ready and leave for the Dill Chahta hai Fort and make plans there to visit Goa daily.

We will also do cycling right at the Para Road (Dear Zindagi).

Visit those aesthetic colorful homes, talk to locals, know their culture and slow life in Goa.

The evening will be at Dauna Paula watching the most beautiful sunset of your life.

End our day in Casino or Tittos lane in Baga beach. Both Entry fees are not included in the package.

Return to Hotel.


### Day 5: South, We are coming !!

Rush To breakfast area.

On our way to South Goa, We will visit Cabo de Rama beach, it's a small hike.

Do Kayaking there.

Overnight stay in south goa.


### Day 6: Last moments

Morning walk near the beach (if waking up early).

We will visit Butterfly Beach and that's it. Trip ends there, and we will leave for our Home.
