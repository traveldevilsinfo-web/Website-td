At Travel Devils, trips aren’t just itineraries. They become inside jokes, louder laughter, stronger friendships, and stories your team will carry long after the bags are unpacked. ## Here’s to the VALUES we strive for

#### College days shape who we become — the values, dreams, and fire that push us to build something bigger than ourselves. Those same values are what brought together a bunch of travel-obsessed explorers who wanted to reinvent how India travels. The outcome? A thriving travel community that today’s youth can truly connect with.

#####   Community Experience

 It is believed that you make connection with an average of 150 people in a lifespan. How much do you think these people offer you to make your time worthy? We’re curating a community where the people you meet add value to your life, through experiences, memories and everything in between. That’s why our community comprise of every person who takes a trip with us, is connected with us on social media, and those who meet us in our everyday lives. The goal is to create a happy impact on whosoever you come across.

#####   Customer Satisfaction

 The very reason Travel Devils was established was because the founders, in their initial phase of career, had experienced what it is to live a mundane 9-5 life. To have been in the shoes of your customers gives a firm understanding of what they need, hence Travel Devils thrives to provide exactly the same, maybe even more! This is the sole reason why we’ve managed to garner more than 3000 reviews across with a rating of 4.9/5 on all social platforms.

#####   Transparency

 How so ironic it may sound, the secret to run a successful business lies in being as transparent as one can be. The teams within Travel Devils, the vendors and the clients maintain an unclouded relationship, and that is why this trio is aligned towards a common goal of nourishing the travel community.

#####   Creative Mindset

 When you’re into a business that dwells on a new adventure everyday, anything regular becomes a setback. So it is justified to say that inventiveness is no less than a drug here at Travel Devils. While the marketing team aces the game, our team captains and even sales executives like to add a spin of creativity to their work! We firmly believe there is no cap to the potential of artistic people, and it surely reflects in the functioning of Travel.

#####   Freedom From Hassles

 If you take a moment to reflect upon how life actually is, it might just strike you hard that Freedom is a rare privilege. Having been constrained to a rulebook of social and work life, we seem to have missed the concept of freedom. At Travel Devils, we strive to make people experience how rawness tastes like. Be it our services, or our work culture, we want people to know that Freedom is the only norm.

## The Story of Travel Devils

      Travel Devils Story

## Our Beginning

Every great journey begins with a dream — and ours started with one too. Back in 2019, Ashwani Singh envisioned a travel company that didn’t just plan trips, but created memories that lingered long after the suitcases were unpacked. That vision gave birth to Travel Devils.

## The Pause

Then 2020 arrived, and the world pressed pause. Travel stopped. Plans froze. But dreams? They didn’t. Travel Devils’s heartbeat remained, quietly waiting for the world to open its doors again.

## A New Beginning

In 2021, three dreamers — Ashwani, Mayank, and Anshuman — joined forces with Amit Jindal, a corporate travel expert. Together, they reignited Travel Devils with a bigger dream and bolder vision.

## Our Vision

We weren’t aiming to be just another travel company. We wanted to make every journey feel destined — whether it’s a team reconnecting at a corporate offsite, friends exploring uncharted lands, or someone ticking off their first international destination.

## Spreading Happiness

That’s how Travel Devils truly took off — spreading happiness through travel, across the world. From international adventures to domestic escapes, from group departures to signature corporate offsites, we’ve grown into a community united by one simple truth: “Travel isn’t just about the places we go, it’s about the people we become along the way.”

## Our Promise

Here’s to the dreamers who built Travel Devils, the travelers who made it their home, and the journeys that still await us. Because with every trip, we’re not just ticking destinations — we’re creating stories worth telling.
