## Frequently Asked Questions

#####   What is Travel Devils?

 Travel Devils brings India’s travellers together through community-based, experiential trips. From solo adventures to squad getaways, we design journeys that hit both the heart and the bucket list.

#####   Who can join Travel Devils trips?

 Anyone! Solo travelers, couples, friends and first-time travelers are all welcome.

#####   Can solo traveler's join group trips?

 Absolutely! Many of our traveler's join solo and make lifelong friends along the way.

#####   What is the group size?

 Group size varies by trip but is usually between 8-50 traveler's.

#####   What is included in the trip package?

 Trip inclusions typically cover accommodation, transportation, sightseeing, trip captain and select meals. Exact inclusions vary by trip.

#####   Is it safe to travel with Travel Devils?

 Safety is our top priority. All trips are led by experienced trip captains and local partners.

#####   Can I customise my trip itinerary?

 Yes! Both domestic and international trips can be tailored to your preferences, travel dates, and budget.

#####   How can I contact Travel Devils for help?

 You can reach us via phone, WhatsApp, email or social media. Our support team is always happy to help.
