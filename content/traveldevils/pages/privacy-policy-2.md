TrippingCube values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and services.

## Information We Collect

- Personal details such as name, phone number, email address
- Booking and travel-related information
- Payment details (processed securely via third-party gateways)
- Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and cookies

## How We Use Your Information

- To process bookings and inquiries
- To communicate trip details, updates, and offers
- To improve our website and customer experience
- For legal and security purposes

## Data Protection

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Your personal information is never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.

## Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party tools (payment gateways, analytics, marketing tools) which have their own privacy policies.
