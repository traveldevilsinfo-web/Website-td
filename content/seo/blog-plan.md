# Travel Devils blog plan (research: 24 Sep 2026)

The search tool runs from the US, so rankings are only a rough stand-in for google.co.in. No keyword volume data was available; difficulty is a relative judgement.

## What the competition does
WanderOn, JustWravel, Thrillophilia and Holidify publish these post types:
- season or "in winter" guides;
- "best time to visit";
- cost breakdowns;
- packing lists;
- trek difficulty guides;
- X vs Y comparisons;
- month- or distance-based listicles.

Their winning posts follow the same pattern:
- 2,500–4,000 words;
- a table of contents;
- tables for costs and seasons;
- a named author and a fresh date;
- one package CTA mid-post, plus related posts at the end;
- FAQs at the end.

Big publishers own the head terms ("weekend getaways from delhi", "best time to visit spiti", "jaisalmer desert camp"). "From Delhi", "N-day itinerary", "cost breakdown", "how to reach" and "in winter" searches are full of small operators and thin blogs. An operator with real trip data can win those.

## Rules we follow
- Business facts come only from our trip data. External facts are checked and dated, with a Sources list and a "Last checked" line.
- No thin listicles, no city-swap copies, no invented quotes, reviews or statistics.
- Every guide links to its trip page (top, middle, end), and trip pages link back ("Read before you go").
- FAQPage markup no longer produces rich results (Google retired them in 2026). FAQs stay on the page for readers and AI answers.
- Update seasonal posts every year. Only bump the year after a real update.

## What will make these rank better (needs the team)
- Real trip photos in each post, captioned with the place and month.
- Named authors: a trip captain or founder with a short bio (Settings → About page: team).
- Real details from trips: road conditions on a given batch, what travellers found hard.
- Refresh seasonal facts each season: temple dates, road openings, fares.
